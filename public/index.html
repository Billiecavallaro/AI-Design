What's an MCP?

Think of it as USB-C for AI. One plug, and any agent can use what's on the other end.

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets AI assistants connect to outside tools and data. Companies use it to plug agents into their software. I used it to plug agents into me.

Why I built it

Plenty of designers talk about agentic AI. This is the receipt.

More and more hiring and buying is starting with an agent rather than a search bar. I'd rather be readable to that agent than invisible to it.

Building this meant designing for two users at once: the person asking, and the AI doing the asking. Every tool name, description and response is a design decision. It's the same work I do every day with AI risk tools, just pointed at myself.
